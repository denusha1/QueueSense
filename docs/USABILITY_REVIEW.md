# Staff and student usability review

## Review status

No staff/student participants or interview notes were available in this workspace on 5 October 2026. The existing patient rating/comment feature measures the clinic visit, not the usability of the QueueSense application. Therefore, this file does not claim user research has happened or that user feedback has been implemented.

## Ready-to-run review

Recruit 3–5 students or staff who have not built the app. Use only the synthetic demo. Ask each person to think aloud while completing these tasks:

1. Sign in using the role demo entry and find the live queue.
2. Check in a synthetic patient and locate the private tracking page.
3. Call, start and complete that token; find its updated patient status.
4. Find department wait-time analytics and explain what the figures mean.
5. Find the model's evaluation result and identify its synthetic-data limitation.
6. Find the audit log and identify who can access it.

For each task, record completion (yes/with help/no), time, misclicks, observed confusion and a short participant quote with consent. Do not collect real patient identifiers or sensitive personal details. Ask participants to rate ease from 1 (difficult) to 5 (easy), then ask: “What was confusing?”, “What did you expect to happen?”, and “What one change would help most?”
